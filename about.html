About me

I'm a data science engineer who builds AI systems that work beyond the notebook: LLM pipelines, multimodal deep learning models and data platforms, designed for real industrial constraints and limited resources.

My training at the Faculty of Sciences of Tunis gave me strong foundations in machine learning, statistics and data modeling. I've since applied them in industry, building retrieval and fine-tuning pipelines with open-source LLMs at Audasoft, leading AI-driven pharmacy automation as founder of Genove, and teaching generative AI and deep learning at GoMyCode and CodeQuest.

I'm now looking for AI and data science engineering roles, as well as PhD opportunities, where I can work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, intelligent systems and process optimization. I care about rigor and impact: well-evaluated models, reproducible work, and data used to tackle real societal challenges.
